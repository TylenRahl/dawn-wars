# Deephold Clans: lore

Ask a Dwarf of the Deephold Clans where his people come from and he will point at the floor. Not at the mountain, at the floor, and further down than that. The Dwarfs say they were the first thing Ostara made, and that the world built them from its own bones to keep the rest of it from falling apart.

## The Stone Debt

Every Dwarf is born owing the mountain. Each ingot of Deepsteel, each block of granite, each seam of coal taken from the dark must be paid back in finished work: a carved stair, a sealed tunnel, a hall that will stand for a thousand years. A clan that takes more than it returns is said to be living on loan, and no clan wants that said of it twice.

Hamrik the delver taught the first clans to dig. Hethra the hearth-mother taught them to keep what they dug. Skarn the wanderer went down a shaft that had no bottom and has not come back, and the bravest Dwarfs still go looking for him. From Hammerdeep, the oldest and deepest hold, King Haldor Ruunsson keeps the accounts of every clan, and knows to the last nail what each one owes the stone.

## The Shield Wall

A Deephold army does not hurry, and it does not need to. Dwarf Warriors in heavy mail lock shields at the centre, while the Greybeards beside them grumble that the young have forgotten how to hold a line and then hold it better than anyone. Ironwalls in Deepsteel plate stand where the fighting is thickest, and the Kingshammers guard their lord with two-handed hammers the size of anvils.

Behind the wall stand the guns. Firebeards fire their long handguns in measured volleys, Boltbeards loose crossbows that never seem to miss, and Embershots carry fire-throwers forged in the deepest furnaces. Miners come up beneath the enemy, Rangers creep in from the hills, and the wild Deathwhirls hurl themselves forward with no armour at all.

## Engines and Runes

The Engineers' guilds keep their secrets close. Their Organ Guns, Flame Cannons and Vengeance Throwers turn any hillside into a fortress, and Rotorwings and the heavy Rotorbomber clatter overhead. The Runesmiths do not cast spells: they cut the Eight Threads into metal, one patient stroke at a time, and a Runelord can make a single hammer outlast a kingdom. The greatest of them carry the Anvil of Ruin to war, and strike it.

## What They Fight For

The Clans fight to keep what is theirs and pay what they owe. The Rukka raid their tunnels, the Gnawkin gnaw beneath them, and the Ashforged, their lost kin, still dig in the burning east. Every hold that falls is a debt the whole people must carry, and the Dwarfs mean to carry every one of them home.
