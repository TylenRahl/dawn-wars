# Ilthari Realms: lore

The Ilthari are the forebears of Void Wars' Athanatoi; the Grey Years they fear here are the Stillness of later ages.

Far out in the western sea, where the Eight Threads come down out of the sky and touch the world, lies the Isle of Ilthar. A ring of peaks, the Crownspire Mountains, stands around its heart, and inside every one of them grows a pale crystal that hums when the Threads pass through. The Ilthari have lived beside that humming since before the other peoples of Ostara learned to count, and they think of themselves as its keepers.

## The Long Watch

Every Ilthari, prince or fisher, owes the isle one year of their life, spent alone in a hut of white stone on a Crownspire ledge, listening to the crystal and smoothing out the knots that form in the Weave when the world below grows violent. Without the Long Watch, the Ilthari say, the Threads would tangle and fray, and every spell in the Sundered Lands would turn to wildfire.

The Ilthari do not age, but they do grow tired. After many centuries an Ilthari may begin to fade into the Grey Years, when colour drains from the world and nothing seems worth the effort of a morning. The Long Watch keeps the Grey away for a while. So does war. Few Ilthari would admit how much the second helps.

## The Ten Realms

Ilthar is not one kingdom but ten, bound under King Calindor in Lumaren, the harbour city of white sails. Pyrrhan breeds dragons in its volcanic valleys. Elvarin raises horses on endless grass. Thessa is a land of cold forest and hunters. Aelsyr is home to the mages, and Sorneth to the quiet tower where swords are taught as a form of prayer. Saelwood shelters the Dawnqueen, the living voice of the mother goddess Siriel. In the north, Umbreth stands empty, its halls shut since the Vhar went away.

## The Host of Ilthar

An Ilthari army is small, old and very precise. Spearmen and Archers in moonsilver hold the line, and the Sea Guard of Lumaren do both at once. Argent Knights ride in a wedge of silver lances, and the Drakeborn of Pyrrhan follow them on the finest steeds or on Dragons. Elvarine Reavers range ahead of the host while Duskblades from the Umbreth border hunt what the Reavers find. The Bladewardens of Sorneth fight with kingsblades as if they were calligraphy brushes. The Ashen Guard tend the fire in the temple of Solantar, and carry its embers into battle in iron lanterns.

## What They Fight For

The Ilthari do not want land. They want the Weave held smooth and the world below kept quiet enough that the crystal can sing. When the Dominion stirs in the north, when the Vhar raid the coasts, or when some foolish mortal wizard pulls a Thread too hard, the white ships leave Lumaren. The Ilthari call it tending. Those they come to tend rarely see the difference.
