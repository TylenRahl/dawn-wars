# Ashforged: lore

The Void Wars Dominion's forges, where flesh is fused with alloy, learned their first craft from the Bindsmiths of Ashmaw.

The Deephold Clans will tell you the Ashforged left. The Ashforged will tell you they were left. Both stories begin in the same place: a far eastern mine, a gate of nine clans, and the morning the mountain above it woke.

## The Burying

When the mountain blew, it sealed the eastern road under a mile of hot stone. Nine clans were trapped beyond it, with their forges, their families and a winter's food. They sent runners west through the ash for help. None came back, and no help came from Hammerdeep. The clans dug, starved and buried their dead in the warm rock, and in the second year they stopped digging.

The ones who lived came to believe the mountain had kept them, the way a bull keeps a herd behind it. They named it the Bull of Ash, and they built Ashmaw in its open throat, a city of furnaces where the air itself is too hot for anyone else to breathe. They have never forgiven the west for not coming, and they have not tried.

## The Bindsmiths

Every Ashforged is a smith, but the Bindsmith Sorcerers work a metal no one else can. They found, in the deep fire of the Bull, that the Weave can be hammered like iron, and that one of the Unmade can be caught and shut inside a forged shell to drive it. Their great works are terrible: the Iron Behemoth, a smoking engine with a bound Unmade for a heart; the Soulfire Cannon; the Kindled Fireborn, living flame in brass armour. Each bargain costs the smith something, and the oldest Bindsmiths are more furnace than flesh.

The Dominion pays dearly for this craft. The black plate of the Sealing is riveted shut by Ashforged hands, and the Dominion lords say no other smith could do it.

## The Furnace Host

An Ashforged army is small, slow and very hard to stop. Ashforged Warriors hold the centre in ranks of axe and blunderbuss. The Furnace Guard, sworn to the Bull, stand at the heart of the line. Bull Centaur Hewers crash in on the flank, led by heroes like Ashtaur, half bull and wholly faithful. Behind them the guns wait: Dreadquake Mortars, Magma Cannons and Deathshrieker Rocket Launchers, ready to turn any field into a furnace.

The Ashforged are too few to fill their own lines, so they buy the rest. Hobgoblin Wolf Riders and Sneakthieves run their errands on contracts no one has ever finished paying off. Iron Rukka Mobs are hired by the season and kept fed on purpose.

## What They Build For

The Ashforged want one thing: that Ashmaw should never again wait on anyone. Every gun they sell and every engine they bind is another brick in the wall around it. Somewhere in the west, Hammerdeep still owes them nine clans. They keep the count.
