# Bloodsworn Courts: lore

East of the Concord Realm lies Morvane, a country of fog, black pines and narrow valleys where the bells ring at dusk and nobody goes out after. The Morvanese pay their taxes in blood, a cupful a year, and they count themselves lucky. Their lords keep the wolves away. Their lords keep everything away.

## The Red Winter

Three hundred years ago a winter came to Morvane that did not end. The harvest froze, the passes closed and the valley starved. The lords of Morvane met in the great hall of Grimhallow to decide who would live, and while they argued, the dead of the valley came up through the snow and walked to the hall doors.

They did not attack. They knocked. The oldest of them, a ploughman buried a century before, set a cup of his own cold blood on the long table and made an offer. The dead would work the fields, guard the passes and fight the lords' wars for ever, if the lords would drink and be bound to the land as the dead were bound. Seven lords drank. Their first was Aurek, of the house of Draeven, and he has not aged a day since.

## The Courts

Each of the seven became a Blood Count, and each keeps a court: a castle, a valley, a household of the living and a much larger household of the dead. The oath cuts both ways. A Count may never leave Morvane for long, and may never let his people starve, for the dead remember who promised them. Count Aurek von Draeven still sits at the head of the long table at Grimhallow. The other Counts plot against him and have never once outlasted him.

## The Risen Host

When a Count goes to war, the ground goes with him. His wizards speak the Invocation of the Risen and the graves open. Skeleton Warriors march in old, patient ranks, and the Gravesworn Guard, the household dead, hold the centre with heavy blades. Pit Ghouls, the living who ate what they should not have in the Red Winter, run ahead on all fours. Barrow Knights ride out on horses that have not breathed in a lifetime, and Bloodsworn Knights, the Counts' own sworn champions, break whatever is left.

Above them come Duskwings, screeching Batgaunts and Bat Swarms thick enough to put out the moon. Gorewolves hunt the flanks. Cursewraiths and the Doomwraiths that lead them pass through shield walls like cold air, and Charnel Wagons creak behind the host, ringing a bell that keeps the dead on their feet.

## What They Fight For

The Courts do not want the Sundered Lands. They want Morvane, whole and theirs, and every border lord who pushes into it learns the same lesson: a Blood Count cannot give up a single field, because the dead are buried there, and he swore.
