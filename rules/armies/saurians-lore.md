# Saurians of the Makers' Jungles: lore

West of the Sundered Lands, across an ocean most captains will not sail, lies Ixmara: a jungle so old that its trees grow on the bones of older trees, and its stepped cities were already ruins when the first human king was crowned. The Saurians who live there did not build those cities. They keep them. To a Saurian that is the more important job.

## The Garden

The Makers came to Ostara before anyone, shaped the jungle of Ixmara as a gardener shapes a bed, and then went away. Nobody knows why. The Saurians believe they were made to tend the garden until the Makers come back, and they take the work with total seriousness. Every terrace is swept, every canal kept clear, every beast where it was put. Anything that was not in the garden when the Makers left is a weed. Weeds are pulled.

## The Last Word

At the heart of every temple city sits a Starseer, vast and ancient and almost motionless, gazing at the sky. The Starseers hold that the Makers left in the middle of speaking, and that the rest of their instruction is written in the slow turning of the stars. A Starseer Mage-Priest may spend a century reading a single line of it. When the sky shifts, the Starseer wakes, says a few quiet words to the Scalelings at its side, and the temple city goes to war. Nobody below asks why. The Last Word is coming, and the garden must be ready for it.

The Saurians honour the Makers' four servants: Zhuatl of the high winds, Ixtaru the serpent, Neqtal of the rivers and Tonaqui of the noon sun. Their priests wear Mindworm Grubs that whisper what the Starseers are thinking.

## The Weeding

Saurian warriors are the heart of every host, broad and scaled and utterly calm, fighting in silent blocks with club and shield. The Shrine Guard stand around the Starseer and have never been seen to step back. Scalelings run, shout and throw javelins, and Chameleon Scalelings vanish into the leaves until it is far too late. Crocbrutes wade through the line swinging weapons no human could lift.

Then come the beasts of the garden. Saurian knights ride Coldscales, and the boldest lords ride a Carnosaur. Shieldbacks lumber forward like moving walls, Quillbacks fire volleys of spines, and Mirecrawlers spit acid from the swamps. Cliffwings and Razorwings fall out of the sky. Salamanders burn, Jungle Swarms bite, and every enemy that leaves Ixmara alive takes the story with them.

## What They Fight For

The Saurians have no wish to rule the Sundered Lands. They want the garden kept. The Gnawkin who tunnel up beneath its cities learn this first, and the Saurians carry a Gnawpelt Banner to show how often. A Saurian does not hate the weed. It simply pulls it.
