# Dominion Hosts: lore

The Dominion of Void Wars in its first age: its law, its Trials and its four powers began here, long before there were machines to inherit them.

North of the last Concord March, past the pine and the bog, lie the Dominion Wastes: grey plains where the snow never quite melts and the sky is never quite still. The land there will not feed everyone, and its people drew one lesson from that. Some are made to rule, and the rest are made to stand beneath them.

## The Four Masteries

The Dominion does not pray. It practises. Its lords teach that there are only four ways one will can master another, and every child of the Wastes is raised on them. Wrath masters by force. Endurance masters by outlasting. Rapture masters by desire, until the ruled beg to be ruled. Cipher masters by knowing what the other does not. A lord who has bent all four to his purpose and belongs to none is called Unbound, and he is the most feared of all.

The Masteries are drawn deep from the Eight Threads, and those who drink too long from one of them are changed. In ages to come the four would be written into the Warden's code. In the Wastes they are still carried in blood.

## The Trials and the Sealing

The tribes of the Wastes fight their lords' wars as Dominion Marauders, wild and fur-clad, and each winter their best warriors are called to the Trials: contests of blade, cold and cunning held before the lords' halls. The tribe whose champion wins eats well that year. The champion is taken away and never comes home.

What waits for him is the Sealing. He is shut inside a suit of black plate, and the smiths rivet it closed while he stands. He will not take it off again. In time he cannot remember his own face. He has become a Dominion Warrior, a single iron purpose that walks. The best are mounted and become Dominion Knights.

## The Host on the March

A Dominion Host is built like a fist. Dominion Warriors are the knuckles. Around them run the Marauders, the Wolfskins and the hounds, with storm-woken Thunderwyrms and Dominion Trolls lumbering behind. Sorcerer Lords throw raw Weave. Soulfire Cannons, dragged by their chained crews, fire burning screams at the walls. Above it all rides an Unmade Lord, a champion his Mastery has finally swallowed whole.

## Why They March

The law of the Dominion is short: *the strong will rules the weak.* Every Host that crosses the border goes south to prove it, against the Concord Realm and its foolish empty chair. A Dominion Warrior does not ask whether his lord is just. He asks only whether his lord is stronger. So far, the answer has always been yes.
