# The Rukka Firms: lore

The same people as Void Wars' Rukka (void-wars/rules/armies/rukkas-lore.md), long before the Signal.

Every people of the Sundered Lands has a story about where the Rukka came from. The Ilthari say they grew out of the mud after a storm. The Deephold Clans say they were dug up. Ask a Rukka and he will look at you as if you were simple. "Came for the match, didn't we."

## The Season

Whatever they once were, the Rukka of Ostara live by the Season. Through the warm months the Firms fight: each other, their neighbours, anyone with a wall worth knocking down. When the weather turns hot they stop, find a bare hillside and lie on it in their thousands, going a deep and alarming orange. Through the cold months they eat potatoes in every form a cook can think of and practise thumping things. Then the Season comes round again, and somebody's kingdom finds out about it.

A Firm is a tribe with its own colours, its own songs and its own Manor, the stretch of land it calls home and the much larger stretch it claims. Firms hate each other with a joyful, lifelong hatred. Only a Gaffer hard enough to flatten every rival can bring them together, and when one does he becomes the Guv'nor, and the Big Ruck begins: a war so loud that whole Marches pack up and leave before it arrives.

## Bosher and Wangler

The Rukka have two gods, and they argue about which is better. Bosher hits things very hard. Wangler hits things very hard when they are not looking. Head Cases, the Rukka's wizards, soak up the roar of a crowd of Ladz until it leaks out of their ears as green fire, and nobody, least of all the Head Case, knows exactly where it will go.

## The Mob

A Rukka army is a crowd that has decided to walk the same way. Rukka Ladz form the heart of it, swinging Choppers and bellowing chants. Iron Rukka, the hardest of the hard, keep everyone else pointed at the enemy. Wild Rukka paint themselves and forget their armour on purpose. Boar Ladz charge on beasts as bad-tempered as their riders.

Around them swarm the Nippers, smaller, quicker and far more crafty, who do the jobs the Rukka think beneath them and the ones the Rukka are too big to understand. Gloom Nippers come up from the caves with Gobblers on leads and Whirlers hidden in their ranks, spinning iron balls on chains. Snivs pedal Pedal Carts nowhere in particular at great speed. Nipper Flingers hurl volunteers, and some who did not volunteer, at the enemy line. Trolls, Giants and the vast Broodmaw spiders follow the noise and the smell of dinner.

## Why They Fight

The Rukka do not want the Sundered Lands. They want a good scrap, a loud song afterwards and a story worth telling at the Local. Any other people would call that madness. The Rukka call it the best day out there is, and they have never once been talked out of it.
