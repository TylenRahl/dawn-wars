# Vhar: lore

The Vhar are the forebears of Void Wars' Thnitoi, and the cure they found in Umbreth is the one the Thnitoi still drink.

Every Ilthari fears the Grey Years, the slow fading that comes to an ageless people when nothing is new any more. In the north of Ilthar, in the misty realm of Umbreth, the Grey once came not to a few tired elders but to everyone at once. Fields went untended. Children stopped laughing. Whole towns sat in silence and waited to turn to stone.

## Ysolde's Cure

A healer of Umbreth named Ysolde found the answer by accident. She was setting a broken arm when the patient screamed, and for one bright moment the Grey lifted from her like fog from a field. She tested it, carefully at first and then not carefully at all. Pain, she learned, was warmth. Fear was colour. Someone else's suffering could fill an empty heart for days.

Within a generation Umbreth was awake again, and rotten to the core. When the other nine realms learned what kept it lively, they gave it a choice: give up the cure or leave. Umbreth left. Its people took ship across the cold sea to the black coasts of Vharnest, named themselves the Vhar, and swore the isle would pay for every year of their exile. Ysolde still rules them from the tower city of Karvesh.

## The Cold Land

Vharnest is a hard country of ice and slate, and the Vhar made it harder. Its cities are fortresses: Karvesh of the hundred towers, Skelmor that watches the north, Vel Karath where the shipwrights work, and Ashvane, city of the Bloodied God, where the streets are swept with red brooms. Out on the water drift the Dread Arks, ships as large as walled towns, which carry raiders to any coast with people worth taking.

The Vhar honour many gods but love the Bloodied God best, for he asks only for what they already enjoy giving.

## The Raid

A Vhar army strikes fast, takes what it came for and leaves before the bells stop ringing. Spearmen and Repeater Crossbowmen form a cold, disciplined core. Dread Ark Corsairs leap from the boats. Coldscale Knights ride great lizards that feel no pain and give much. Lashrunner Chariots run down anyone who flees, and Hexfire Warlocks ride with them, throwing curses as they go.

Then come the faithful. Bloodmaidens, the chosen of the Bloodied God, fight laughing with poisoned knives. The Executioners of Ashvane swing their long blades in perfect rhythm, led by a Greatblade Master. An Assassin of the Bloodied God may already be standing behind the enemy general. Beastmasters drive Hydras, Harpies and the dreaded Abyssmaw out of the cages, and the Bloodmire Shrine rolls forward with a gaze that stops hearts.

## What They Fight For

The Vhar want Ilthar back, but they want the screaming more. Every raid fills the cold halls of Karvesh with captives, and every captive keeps the Grey away a little longer.
