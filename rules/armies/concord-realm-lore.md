# The Concord Realm: lore

The Concord Realm is the ancestor of Void Wars' Concord, and its founding oath is the seed of the Pledge.

Long before anyone looked up at the stars and thought of going there, the Concord Realm was a ragged quilt of river towns, forest holds and quarrelling lords at the heart of the Sundered Lands of Ostara. Each valley had its own king, and every king was at war with the next one. Then came Halvar, a smith's son from the Rhenmark, who did the one thing no warlord had tried. He did not conquer his neighbours. He asked them.

## The Compact

Halvar rode from hall to hall with an empty chair lashed to his saddle. At every hall he set it down and made the same offer: sit with me as an equal, keep your own laws, and when one of us is threatened, all of us march. A lord could refuse, and many did. A lord could also leave the Compact one day, if he gave his reasons in the open. Halvar held that a promise no one is free to break is not a promise at all, only a chain.

Eleven lords sat in the chair. Their lands became the first Marches, and their heirs are the March Counts. When Halvar died defending the ford at Halvarsburg, the Counts carried his hammer, the Kingsmaul, to the next High King they chose, and the people began to call him Saint Halvar. Each Count still swears the Compact again on taking office, holding an Oathblade over the empty chair that stands in the High King's hall. Aldric III, the present High King, has sworn it four times and broken it never.

## The Realm at War

The Realm has no single army. It has many, raised by the Marches and brought together when the call goes out. Its strength is the ordinary soldier: March Troops in their Count's colours, halberd and spear shoulder to shoulder, handgunners and crossbowmen behind them. Kingsblades guard the banners. The great knightly orders, the Sunburst, the Ember Heart, the Winter Fang, the Leopard Knights and the grim Knights of the Pale Keeper, ride where the line needs breaking. The richest of them go to war on Clawsteeds.

Kessel's foundries supply what courage cannot. Its engineers cast cannon, build Thunderclap Volley Guns and Skyfire Rocket Batteries, and sometimes send out an Iron Kettle, a riveted steam engine of war that no two engineers agree how to repair. The Realm's wizards study the Eight Threads under the High King's licence. The priests of Saint Halvar march in the front rank, and in the northern Marches of Vargmark and Vargsheim men still pray to Varg the wolf for a hard winter and a harder fight.

## What They Fight For

The Realm is under siege from every side: the Rukka from the hills, the Bloodsworn Courts from Morvane, the Hornkin from the deep woods and the Dominion from the north. It survives because its people have chosen it. A March Troop who holds the line is not holding it for a king. He is holding it for the chair, and for the promise that someone will come when he calls.
