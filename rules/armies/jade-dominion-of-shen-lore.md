# Jade Dominion of Shen: lore

Far to the east of the Sundered Lands, past deserts and mountains that swallow armies, lies the Jade Dominion of Shen. It is the largest realm on Ostara, the oldest that still keeps its own records, and the only one that has ever counted every person living in it. The Shenese are very proud of the count. They do it again every nine years.

## The Dragon Throne

Shen is ruled by the Dragon Throne, and the people speak of the Throne, not of whoever sits on it. The seat itself is carved from a single Dragon's tooth, and the ruler takes no name of his own while he holds it. Under the Throne serve ten thousand Lord Magistrates, each with a seal, a river to keep clean and a granary to keep full. A magistrate whose granary runs empty in a good year does not remain a magistrate for long.

Shen believes that a realm, like a river, must be kept moving or it will rot. Every canal is dredged and every harvest weighed. The Astromancers watch the Eight Threads cross the night sky and set the calendar by them, and the Lianshi, the court's wizards, learn the Weave as a craft, by rote and patient practice. Some of the eldest Lianshi are whispered to be Dragons wearing human faces. Mei Lan, the most famous of them, has never troubled to deny it.

## The Nine Gates

Any child in Shen may sit the Nine Gates, a test of arms, letters and nerve held each spring in every province. Those who pass become Jade Warriors, the standing soldiers of Shen, armoured in green lacquer and drilled until a hundred of them move like one. Jade Lancers are the best riders among them. Those who fail go home with honour and march, when called, in the Peasant Levy, whose spears and banners fill the Jade lines by the thousand.

## Shen at War

A Shenese army is planned before it marches. The Strategist chooses the ground, often weeks ahead. Iron Hail Gunners fire in ranks that never break rhythm, and Crane Gunner Teams pick out officers at long range. Fire Rain Rocket Batteries fill the sky with streaks of flame, and the Grand Cannon answers anything that survives. Above them floats the Kite Lantern, a great paper balloon that drops fire on the enemy below. Shenese Sentinels, clay giants waking at a Lianshi's word, guard the gates and the guns, and the Great Spirit Longma, a heavenly horse, carries the bravest commanders into the fight.

## What They Fight For

Shen fights for order. To the Shenese the rest of the Sundered Lands is a flood plain of rival kingdoms and Shen is the dyke. When the Dominion Hosts or the Rukka come over the passes, the Throne does not ask who is to blame. It asks what the repair will cost, and it sends an army to pay it.
