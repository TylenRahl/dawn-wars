# Gutlords: lore

High in the eastern mountains, past the last road, the ground cracks open into a gorge so deep that no rope has reached the bottom. Warm air rises out of it day and night, and with the air comes a sound like an enormous stomach turning over. The Ogres who live around its rim call it the Hunger Below. They are the Gutlords, and they have been feeding it for as long as there have been Ogres.

## The First Meal

The Gutlords tell it like this. Before the world, there was only hunger. The hunger ate the dark and made the sky, ate the sky and made the ground, and when it was full it lay down beneath the mountains and slept. It is waking now, a little more each year, and when it wakes properly it will want feeding. Anything that is not food by then will become food.

So the Gutlords make sure they are the ones doing the eating. Size is holiness. A big belly is a full temple. An Ogre who can swallow a whole ox is respected, and one who can swallow a whole army is worshipped.

## The Tables

A Gutlord tribe is called a Table, after the long slab of rock where it sits down to eat, and its ruler is the Tyrant, simply the biggest Ogre who has not yet been beaten. Each Table throws a share of every hunt and every battle into the gorge, and its Gorgemaster, priest and cook in one, reads the rumbles that come back. The most famous Tyrant of all, Big Gorm, once marched his Table across the Wyrdstone Desert for a bet, and came back with fewer Ogres and a great deal more to say about it.

When the mountains run short of meat, the Tables go down. Gutlords hire out to the Concord Realm, the Broken Crowns and the Jade Dominion of Shen, who pay in coin, beer and Shenese jade. They are honest mercenaries: they stay bought until the food runs out.

## The Charge

A Gutlord army is not subtle. Ogre Bulls in iron gut-plates lead the charge and hit like a landslide. Ironbellies, the Tyrant's own guard, follow in heavier plate. Shotbellies carry cannon on their shoulders. Frostfang riders thunder in on great shaggy beasts, while bigger beasts still, the Horncrusher, the Cragback and the frost-breathing Rimetusk, shake the ground. Fangcats run in packs, Snowstalkers come down with the blizzard and Gulpers burst up from the ground behind the enemy line.

Around the Ogres' feet scurry the Grublings, small, sly and endlessly put upon, who carry the bags, load the Gutcannon, wind the Junkflinger and shout "Look Out Below!" as Giants fall on their friends.

## What They Fight For

Dinner. Then the next one. And, somewhere far beneath them all, the one that is coming.
