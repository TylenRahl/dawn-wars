# The Unmade: lore

The Unmade of Void Wars were once people reshaped into weapons; in Ostara the Weave does that work, ages before the Dominion's forges learn to copy it.

Every Unmade was once somebody. That is the first thing a Concord priest teaches a frightened recruit, and the last thing the recruit wants to hear. The thing that comes at him with a blade of red iron had a mother. It may even have had his face.

## How a Person Is Unmade

The Dominion teaches four Masteries, four ways for one will to rule another: Wrath, Endurance, Rapture and Cipher. Each is a current in the Eight Threads, and each is hungry. A warrior who draws on one long enough is slowly filled by it. First it takes his habits, then his memories, then his name. When nothing is left but the Mastery itself, the Weave gives it a body to match, and a new Unmade steps out of the old one.

The Dominion calls this an honour. Its champions chase it on purpose, and the greatest of them become Unmade Lords and still give orders. Most are not so lucky. Most simply vanish into what they wanted, and keep wanting it forever.

## The Realm of the Dominion

Beyond the Dominion Wastes the Threads tangle into a storm that never breaks, and inside it lies the Realm of the Dominion. No map shows it and no traveller has walked out of it. The Unmade gather there in their thousands, sorted by their Masteries the way iron sorts by weight. Whenever the Threads run thick over the Sundered Lands, after a great battle, a comet or a mass grave, the storm leans south, and the Unmade walk through.

## The Four Hosts

Each Mastery brings its own shape of war. Wrath sends the Gorehewers in tight red ranks, with Flesh Hounds running ahead and Ironcrushers riding brass beasts behind, and above them the winged Bloodtyrant. Endurance sends the Rotbearers, who cannot be hurried and cannot be stopped, a tide of giggling Blightlings around their feet, and the vast Great Blighted One rolling after. Rapture sends the Enthrallers, quick and lovely and cruel, with Seekers on long-legged mounts and the Keeper of Delights smiling over all of it. Cipher sends the Gleeful Horrors, who laugh as they cast fire and leave Sullen Horrors behind when they fall, and the Lord of Riddles, who knows how the battle ends and will not say.

Where all four march together, the Soul Mill comes too: a great engine of iron and Weave, grinding forward on legs, the closest the Unmade come to a thing that was built.

## What They Want

The Unmade do not want land or gold. Each wants only its Mastery, made larger. Wrath wants the fight, Endurance the long wearing down, Rapture the surrender, Cipher the secret. They are sent against the Concord Realm for one reason above all. Its soldiers know what the Unmade used to be, and the Dominion is counting on it to break them.
