Keys

Click a piece to select it, drag to move it. Q / E turn it 15° (with Shift 5°). [ / ] narrower / wider, - / = shallower / deeper. Arrows nudge it ½". D duplicates, Delete removes. Esc or right-click lets go. Mouse wheel zooms, right-drag pans, Home shows the whole table, Shift + drag measures.

Blue deploys along the near (bottom) edge, red along the far edge, 12" deep. The pale circle is 12" round the centre: the recommended set-up keeps terrain out of it.
